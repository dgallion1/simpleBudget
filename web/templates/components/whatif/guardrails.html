Drop/rise guardrails (simple)

Automatically adjust spending based on portfolio performance.

Cuts withdrawal when portfolio drops from peak; raises withdrawal when portfolio rises above baseline. This is a simplified guardrail — not the four-rule Guyton-Klinger (2006) model.
