Spending guardrails

These rules adjust your living-expense budget. Healthcare, property tax, and other expense lines are not automatically cut. An optional real-dollar floor sets a minimum living-expense request; it does not guarantee funding. This is a simplified guardrail — not the four-rule Guyton-Klinger (2006) model.

Cuts the living-expense budget when the portfolio drops from peak; raises it when the portfolio rises above baseline.
