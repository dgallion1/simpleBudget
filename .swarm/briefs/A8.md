### Scope

Three additions. No new balance, projection or classification logic.

1. **Accounts card on the dashboard** — per account: name, institution,
   current balance, and data freshness ("data through Aug 12"). Render each
   state distinctly:
   - **healthy** — balance available and above threshold;
   - **low** — below its low-balance threshold. Flag it with an icon **and
     text**, never color alone (ACCESSIBILITY.md point 8);
   - **stale** — the account's latest transaction is old. A stale CSV must not
     be able to masquerade as a healthy balance, so say so visibly;
   - **no anchor** — the balance is *unavailable*. Render that as unknown, NOT
     as `$0.00`. A zero balance and an unknown balance are different facts and
     conflating them could send the user to move money they do not need to.
2. **Projection summary line** — for checking/savings accounts, one line from
   `Project`: when the balance is projected to cross below the threshold and
   the suggested top-up, e.g. "drops below $500 around Sep 3 — you usually
   move $2,000". If there is no crossing, say the account is projected to stay
   above its threshold. If the projection is unavailable (no anchor), say that
   rather than showing a number.
3. **Unassigned-files banner** — on the dashboard *and* the explorer: when
   `DataLoader.UnassignedCount()` is non-zero, show how many transactions came
   from CSVs matching no account, linking to `/accounts`. Files are never
   silently dropped, and this banner is what makes that visible.
