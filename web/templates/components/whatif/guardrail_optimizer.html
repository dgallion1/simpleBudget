About these simulations

These are the best choices found in the searched settings, not a global optimum. Candidates must meet your target on an independent validation sample.

{{.Optimizer.SearchRuns}} search simulations; {{.Optimizer.ValidationRuns}} independent validation simulations per policy, using identical scenario draws for each comparison. Horizon: {{.Optimizer.HorizonMinYears}}–{{.Optimizer.HorizonMaxYears}} years, including longevity variation.

Funded living spending conservatively pays other obligations first: requested living spending minus any total funding shortfall, with a minimum of zero. Only funded spending counts toward lifetime living spending.

Outcomes use cumulative CPI to express money in today's dollars, continue through depletion for the full simulated horizon, and count spending below the minimum at cent precision. Annual cuts compare consecutive complete 12-month funded spending totals.
